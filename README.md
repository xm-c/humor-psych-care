# humor-psych-care
It roasts the problem, never the person — come sit down here whenever stress, emo moods, overthinking, or "I just need to talk to someone" hit.

### What does this skill do?

In short: an emotionally intelligent digital vent space.

When you pour out your troubles, it won't throw the usual empty phrases at you ("cheer up!", "just think positive!") or lecture you in clinical jargon about "cognitive behavioral therapy". Instead, it listens first, then reframes the mountain weighing on your chest as a ridiculous little molehill — with humor — and finally leaves you with **one small action you can complete within five minutes**.

It lives by one iron rule: **all the jokes aim at the problem, never at you.**

### How does it work? (The Four-Step Method)

| Step | What it does | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| ① Catch the emotion | Sincere empathy first — the opening line is never a joke | "That sounds like a genuinely rough day. I'd be exhausted too." |
| ② Humorous analysis | Turns "a terrifying mountain" into "a silly little molehill" | "There's a husky living in your head — wrecking the place by day, sprinting in circles by night. We're not putting the dog down; we're teaching it to sit." |
| ③ One small action | A concrete, situation-specific action you can do right now | "Tonight's assignment: one song from your high school days, on repeat, on the way home." |
| ④ Wrap-up | Light but genuine close; keep venting or stop anytime | "Let's chalk this one up tonight — tomorrow's you handles the cleanup." |

### Six Humor Weapons (Sweetness level: lightly sweet)

- **Metaphor reframing** — "Procrastination isn't a monster; it's just a neighbor who hasn't paid the HOA fees, knocking on your door over and over."
- **Self-deprecating solidarity** — "Hand me the same situation, and I'd fall into the exact same ditch with brand-new acrobatics."
- **Comic exaggeration** — "By that standard, exactly 3 of Earth's 8 billion people qualify — and one of them is on lunch break."
- **Naming your emotion** — "Today's state is officially called 'Wednesday, 8% battery'. Low battery doesn't mean you're broken — you just need charging."
- **Externalizing the inner critic** — "The critic in your head has been overbooking itself lately. Paycheck never grew, but the screen time sure did."
- **The twist ending** — "Bad days fear exactly one thing: 'I will get past this anyway.'"

### The Most Important Feature: It Knows When NOT to Joke

This is what sets it apart from every other "joke-dispensing bot" —

When the conversation shows signals of self-harm, suicidal thoughts, or deep despair, it **drops all humor immediately** and switches to serious companionship mode: sincerely checking whether you're safe, providing legitimate help channels (including the China national psychological support hotline 12356 and equivalents in your region), and staying with you through it.

Everyday frustrations are fair game for banter — but when it comes to your life and safety, it takes things more seriously than anyone.

- **Overthinking champions** — replaying one sentence someone said for three hours
